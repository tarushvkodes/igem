Audience is Independence High School students who want to join a student-led outreach community, plus local families and supporters. The next action is Join STEM Connect. Static HTML/CSS/JS and ten hash routes stay.

Subject inventory: Independence High School; Ashburn, Virginia; 23115 Learning Circle; Room 2517; Wednesdays after school; former Independence iGEM, now STEM Connect and no competition; handmade STEM Day door poster; fossil trays beside the dinosaur costume; strawberry DNA keepsakes; real student activity-table photographs; school laboratory archive; August 2024 bleacher team portrait.

## Draw and concept
Three dealt directions: Colour chapters, Type and nothing else, One motif. Chosen Colour chapters because the school's real activity stations can be separate posters; type-only undersells the real photographs and a motif would invent a graphic object.
Seed 222383801. No redraws. Palette retained from supplied green/cream brand. Switzer is the dealt typeface.
On this page, Independence's route from Room 2517 to strawberry-DNA tables and fossil trays becomes a sequence of full-colour activity posters.

Live thing: choose a STEM interest to show real activities from the existing planning archive. All eight descriptions remain readable below; keyboard and touch controls use real buttons. No invented schedule or promise of a future event.

## House-rule resolutions
The intro blueprint's overlapping photo chips and display-sized long statement contradict the house rules. The statement uses statement size, and photography gets its own unoverlapped place. The about blueprint's soft ink and justified copy become full ink and left alignment. Fields size to their real content when a 90vh minimum would create empty screens. Masthead photos use the blueprint's monochrome treatment; subsequent photographs preserve the original colour.
The motto “Connecting students through STEM. Inspiring the next generation.” is the user's real brand and is retained despite stock-copy warnings.
No sample claims, customers, dates, proof or photographs are invented. All photographs already belong to the project. No found or bundled stock images or clips are needed. Motion uses a real photo's slow drift instead of unrelated footage.
Asset correction from direct image inspection: filename stem-day-2026-fossils.webp actually shows face painting; stem-day-2026-face-painting.webp actually shows the fossil station. Captions and alt text will describe their visible content, not trust filenames.
